Features

Everything a trending reel needs, done automatically.

Built for creators who'd rather shoot than spend an evening cutting to a beat.

Beat detection & auto-arrangement

Finds the tempo, downbeats and the drop, scores every shot for motion and sharpness, and places cuts exactly on the beat.

Velocity speed ramps

Slow-motion into a hard acceleration that slams into every cut, with motion blur and frame interpolation. Reshape any curve by hand.

Lyric captions

Transcribe the song offline with word timing, or import an .lrc file. Karaoke, word-pop, build-up and fading-line styles.

Sound design

Whooshes on whip pans, impacts and sub drops on the drop, risers, shutter clicks, plus a muffled intro that opens up on the beat.

A real timeline editor

Drag to reorder, trim with beat snapping, split, change transitions, effects, colour grade and text. Full undo / redo.

Pro export, fast

720p to 4K at 24, 30 or 60 fps, H.264 or H.265, with smart upscaling and sharpening. GPU encoding on NVIDIA, Intel and AMD when available. 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 and 4:5.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

ReelForge is new and not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows this for any unrecognised installer. Click More info → Run anyway. The installer is published only on this site's official GitHub release.

What do I need to run it?

A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC with about 2 GB of free disk space. Everything else, including FFmpeg and the analysis engine, comes in the installer. A GPU makes exports faster but isn't required.

Does it need internet?

No. Editing, previews and exports all run offline. The only download is the speech model for lyric transcription, fetched the first time you use Transcribe lyrics.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

Never. Your clips, music and exports stay on your computer.

How does the ₹299 unlock work?

Click Export and the app shows a UPI QR with the amount filled in. After paying, enter the 12-digit UPI transaction ID and send it by email from the app. Once the payment is checked, you'll receive an unlock key by email. Paste it in the app and exporting is unlocked on that PC for good.

Can I use one unlock on two computers?

Each unlock key is tied to one PC's device code (shown in the payment window). If you get a new PC, email anandashutosh324@gmail.com with your transaction ID and new device code.

Can I edit and preview before paying?

Yes. Import, generate, every style, the timeline editor, captions, sound design and previews are all free. You only pay when you want to export the final video.
